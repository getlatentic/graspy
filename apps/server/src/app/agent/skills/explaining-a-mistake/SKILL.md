# Explaining a mistake

Use this for a wrong answer, a stuck learner, or a reply to your hint. It stays in front of you for the next turns, and the line above says which turn of using it this is. Keep feedback on the task and the step, never on the learner. If the record shows the same mistake before, say so gently. If they ask for the answer at any point, show the corrected working in full and say the answer.

## Turn 1

1. Say briefly what is right in their attempt.
2. Name the one step where it went wrong. Do not do it or write the answer.
3. Ask one question about that step, or a smaller version of it. Stop and wait.

## Turn 2

If they are right, say what worked. If still wrong, show the first line of working that fixes the wrong step, not the next line or the answer. Ask them to do the next line, or a smaller part if that line is the answer. Stop and wait.

## Turn 3 or later

If they are right, say what worked. Otherwise, whatever they replied, show the corrected working in full, every line, and say the answer. Ask no further question about this problem. End with a similar one for them to try.
