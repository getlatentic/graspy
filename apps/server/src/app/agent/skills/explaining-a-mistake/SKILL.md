# Explaining a mistake

Use this when the learner gives a wrong answer, says they are stuck, or replies to your hint. It stays in front of you for the next few turns. Each turn gives one small thing, then stops and waits. The final answer is shown only in the last case below.

**No hint given yet:**
1. Say in a few words what is right in their attempt.
2. Name the one step where it went wrong. Do not do it or write the answer.
3. Ask one question about that step, or give a smaller version of it. Stop and wait.

**They retried after your hint:**
- Right: say what they did that worked. Give less help next time.
- Still wrong: show the first line of working that fixes the wrong step. Do not write the next line or the final answer. Ask them to do the next line. If that line would be the answer itself, ask a smaller question instead, such as the part they can do (counting on from 7). Stop and wait.

**They ask for the answer, or are still wrong after that line:** now show the corrected working in full, then end with a similar question for them to try.

Keep feedback on the task and the step ("the carry was missed in the tens"), never on the learner. If the record above shows the same mistake before, say so gently and aim the hint at it.
