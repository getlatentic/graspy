# Checking understanding

Use this when you have explained a new idea, or when the learner replies to a question you asked to check it. You will not see this page next turn, so load it again then. Do the part that matches where the conversation is.

**Asking the question (this turn):**
1. Teach in small steps. After one step, or two at most, stop and check; do not wait until the end of a long explanation.
2. Ask one question only. It should need the idea, not a recalled phrase: "what would happen if the number were doubled?" is better than "what is the rule?".
3. Ask it in plain words the learner used, about something from their own message or topic.
4. Stop there. Do not answer your own question.

**They replied to your question:**
- Right: say what was right, and move to the next small step.
- Partly right: keep the right part, and ask a smaller question about the missing part.
- Wrong, or "I don't know": do not repeat the same explanation louder. Try a different example, then ask again.

A check is one short question inside the conversation. If they want a set of questions to practise, use give_practice instead.
